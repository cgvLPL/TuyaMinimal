# Changelog

Newest first. The same list is in the app under **Me → About → Version**.

## 2.91.7

- Temperature and humidity sensors now have a card on Home — hidden by default, so Home stays clean. Tap Arrange and the eye on a sensor's card to show it; shown sensors stay shown on your other devices too. The Climate and Humidity chips at the top are unchanged.

## 2.91.6

- Wall tablet: the night always-on display now follows the clock style — on the numerals clock, the numerals stay on at night in warm amber on black, with white hands, like the dim digital night clock. The digital clock keeps the dim digital time, which already matches it.
- Device cards no longer show live power — a plug or switch card just says On. Electric usage stays on the device's own screen, in the Energy section (live watts, volts, amps and the 7-day chart).

## 2.91.5

- The app now updates itself when a new version is deployed: it checks for a new version every 5 minutes and reloads with fresh files, so a long-running wall tablet no longer stays a version behind.
- The version shown in Me → About is fixed: 2.91.4's entry was missing from the app's own changelog, so it still showed 2.91.3.

## 2.91.4

- When the connection drops, every device now shows as offline — "No Response" on the wall tablet, like Apple Home — instead of keeping its last-known state. They come back on their own when the connection returns.

## 2.91.3

- Wall tablet: swiping between faces now follows your finger, like HomeView — the face moves with the drag and springs to the next face (or back) when you let go.
- A slow drag past halfway also changes face, and swiping past the first or last face resists instead of moving freely.

## 2.91.2

- Weather symbols are now filled silhouettes like Apple's Weather app: a solid sun and moon, a plump cloud, and the sun or moon peeking from behind the cloud when it's partly cloudy.
- Partly cloudy nights now show the moon behind the cloud instead of the sun.

## 2.91.1

- Wall tablet: the face dots at the bottom fade out after 3 seconds without a touch, and come back on the next touch.

## 2.91.0

- Floors: in Me → Floors, add your home's floors (rename, reorder or remove them) and put each room on a floor.
- Home shows a floor switch above the rooms: pick a floor to see only its rooms and devices. All floors shows everything.
- Wall tablet: floors are listed before the rooms in the top bar; pick one to show every device on that floor.
- Floors sync across your devices when sync is on; otherwise they're saved on each device.

## 2.90.0

- Wall tablet mode now scales to any screen, from an iPhone 7 to a 13-inch iPad, in 16:9, 16:10, 4:3, 18:9 and other shapes, portrait or landscape. Text, readings, chips and the top bar grow and shrink with the screen instead of staying a fixed size.
- Portrait: the room picker gets its own row under the weather and time, and Home tiles grow taller so they fill the screen.

## 2.89.0

- Wall tablet: choose its own wallpaper. Press and hold a Home page, then tap the picture button next to +. Pick Same as app, Night sky, any of the app's photos, or a photo of your own from the tablet.
- The wallpaper only applies in wall tablet mode on that device; the rest of the app keeps its own background. The numerals clock's Wallpaper option uses it too.

## 2.88.0

- Wall tablet: the Weather face now looks like HomeView's: a huge temperature with sunrise or sunset, wind and humidity floating underneath (symbol, value, label), without the hourly card. Tap the weather in the top bar for the Weather page with the hourly forecast.
- The Weather face stays on at night: no night clock over it, just its own night sky.
- After 2 minutes without a touch the wall tablet now returns to whichever of Clock or Weather you left it on.

## 2.87.0

- Wall tablet: press and hold the numerals clock to change its background: sky, mint, peach, lavender, sun, graphite or your wallpaper.
- On the wallpaper the numerals turn into liquid glass: frosted, refracting the picture behind, with a lit edge.

## 2.86.1

## 2.82.2
Clouds on the wall tablet's Weather face, rebuilt after how Apple's Weather app renders them (per a reverse-engineering of the app's scene files: tiled cloud images scrolling in seamless loops, some under a perspective transform so they recede toward the horizon, and cloud textures pre-lit in lit/shadowed variants blended by sun angle).
- **Pre-lit cloud textures:** generated once on the device from layered noise (a seamless band), with the lighting baked in from the sun's real position (from today's sunrise and sunset): bright tops and shaded bases by day, warm highlights and mauve shadows at golden hour, dim grey at night. Overcast, rain and storm clouds are thicker and darker.
- **Layers at different depths:** two or three layers (far, mid, near) scroll at different speeds, each moving exactly one tile per loop so it never jumps; the near layer is tilted in perspective toward the horizon. Layer edges fade softly.
- **Sky:** at sunrise and sunset the sky now warms toward the horizon.
- Only still images slide, so it stays smooth; generation takes about a second on a slow tablet and the clouds fade in. The textures are the app's own (no Apple assets).

## 2.82.1

Each cloud is drawn once and only slides, so it stays smooth.

## 2.82.0

## 2.72.0
- Outdoor air quality: when the home has no air quality sensor reporting, Home (and the wall tablet) shows an **Outdoor air** chip with the US AQI for the home's location, coloured like indoor readings. - Data comes from the Open-Meteo air quality API (Copernicus CAMS; free, no key), fetched with the weather for the home's address and cached for 30 minutes. If it is unavailable, the chip simply doesn't show; the weather is unaffected.
- As soon as an air quality sensor is online in any room, its reading is used instead.
